# Direct offline voice call
Uses the phone's built-in Android Opus encoder and decoder (Android 10+), 48 kHz mono at 24 kbps in 20 ms frames, carried only inside the pinned mutual-TLS Wi-Fi Direct session. Nothing extra is installed. Echo cancellation and noise suppression are switched on where the phone has them. During a call the Call tab shows the duration and Mute (sends silence, the link stays up) and Speaker on/off. One hop only; no relay, conference or background calling yet.

## Call quality and adaptive bitrate
During a call the Call tab shows, once a second, only measured values: round-trip time (ping/pong inside the call every 2 s), current bitrate, playback delay, share of frames dropped and playback gaps. Bitrate starts at 24 kbps and moves between 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32 kbps: one step down as soon as a round trip exceeds 400 ms, one step up after three in a row under 150 ms (the encoder restarts, a short click is possible). The transport is TCP inside TLS, so network loss shows up as delay and bursts; the receiver drops frames instead of letting playback delay grow beyond 120 ms. Each call's summary (duration, average and maximum round trip, drops, gaps, bitrate changes) is saved on the phone; the last 10 are listed under RECENT CALLS and can be copied. Both phones need this version (voice protocol 0x4e4c0302); an older phone gets a clear version error.

## Link and ringing
When both phones are in one Wi-Fi Direct group, each has the other's contact selected and Auto relay is off, the app opens an always-on pinned TLS link (Link.kt). Keepalives every 3 s detect a dead link within 10 s; it reconnects while the group exists and stops after 3 minutes without a handshake. Over the link: Call rings the other phone (ringtone, Accept, Decline; a notification when the app is in the background), unanswered calls stop after 45 s, a busy phone declines; queued nearby messages are exchanged on connect and on every Send nearby; Exchange messages and Mesh relay sync run over the link. The link stays up while the app is in the background, but Android may still kill the app; there is no foreground call service yet. The Run authenticated probe button is blocked while linked.

## Two-phone acceptance
Form a group, select each other's contact, grant microphone access. Wait for Linked on both. Tap Call on either phone: the other must ring; Accept: both must hear each other. Decline: caller sees Declined. No answer: both stop after 45 s. Tap Hang up on either phone: both calls must end and the link must come back. Send nearby while linked: the other phone shows the message without tapping anything. Switch Wi-Fi off on one phone: the other shows Link lost within 10 s. Wrong fingerprint: no audio. Phone without Opus codec: clear error. Record latency, dropouts and range per phone model. No physical results recorded.
